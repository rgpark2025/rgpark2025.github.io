Rules (25/11/10): Round 1 → Dev = 0.10×Growth + 0.02×Green; Em = max(0, 0.015×(Growth − Green)). If Social < 25: Dev −= 0.5. Rounds 2–4 → Dev += 0.09×Growth + 0.04×Green; Em += max(0, 0.015×(Growth − Green))×(1+0.07×(round−1)); Happiness each round = Dev − Em − ShockImpact + 0.012×Social + 0.012×Green, after shock damage is applied to Dev. Final Round 5 → Dev += 0.04×Growth + 0.06×Green; Em += same formula; Happiness = Dev − Em − 3×ShockImpact + 0.01×Social + 0.012×Green, after shock damage. Climate Risk updates every round: Risk += α×Em − γ×(0.6×Green + 0.4×Social), floored at 0 (default α=2.8, γ=0.007). Shock scale (Rounds 2–5): Risk<0.8 ⇒ mild; Risk<2.0 ⇒ moderate; else severe. ShockImpact is a random draw within its band, reduced by adaptation: Impact = Base × RoundMultiplier × (1 − 0.003×Social) × (1 − 0.0025×Green), bounded below at 0.3. RoundMultiplier = [—, —, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 4.0]. Growth>Green also nudges shocks upward. Shock damage to the economy (Rounds 2–5): after Impact is realized, Development is reduced by Damage = Impact × DamageMultiplier, with DamageMultiplier = [—, —, 0.5, 0.8, 1.0, 2.0].
